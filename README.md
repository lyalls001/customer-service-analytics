# Customer Service Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project exploring repeat customer contacts and operational performance using synthetic customer service data.

## 🎯 Business Problem

A customer service organization is experiencing a high volume of repeat contacts.

Management wants to understand why customers contact the organization multiple times and identify opportunities to improve first-contact resolution, customer experience, and operational efficiency.

## ❓ Business Questions

This project aims to answer the following questions:

- How high is the repeat contact rate?
- Which contact reasons generate the most repeat contacts?
- Which channels show the highest repeat contact rates?
- How does first-contact resolution affect repeat contacts?
- How much workload is generated by repeat contacts?
- Are there patterns in handling time, teams, channels, or contact reasons?
- Where are the largest opportunities for process improvement?

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators

The analysis will focus on:

- Total Contacts
- Unique Customers
- Repeat Contact Rate
- First Contact Resolution Rate
- Average Contacts per Customer
- Average Handling Time
- Repeat Contacts by Channel
- Repeat Contacts by Contact Reason

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses a fully synthetic customer service dataset created specifically for this portfolio project.

No proprietary or employer data is used.

The dataset will simulate realistic customer service interactions including:

- Customer ID
- Contact ID
- Contact Date & Time
- Contact Channel
- Contact Reason
- Team
- Handling Time
- Resolution Status
- First Contact Resolution
- Repeat Contact Indicator
